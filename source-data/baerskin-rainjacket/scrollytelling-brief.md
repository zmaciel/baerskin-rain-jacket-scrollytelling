## Scrollytelling Arc

1. Sudden rain ruins plans.
   Show the outdoor problem immediately: weather turns fast, normal jackets soak through, and gear gets wet.

2. The jacket seals the weak points.
   Explain waterproof rating, taped seams, silicone-sealed zips, storm flap, and BÆR-Tex coating as one protection system.

3. It stays packable and mobile.
   Move from heavy-duty waterproofing to the product tension: it still folds into its own pocket and stays light/flexible.

4. It adapts to real use.
   Use the adjustable hood, waist, wrist cuffs, five pockets, and 3-in-1 hoodie zip-in system as the practical middle chapters.

5. Proof, fit, and offer.
   Close with reviews, size guidance, color/size breadth, 60-day returns, free shipping cues, and the current offer.
